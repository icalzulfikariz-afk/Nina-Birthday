To Nina Tutachia,

Happy 17th birthday! ♡ In Indonesia, it feels a little strange to say “I love you” because the phrase is often associated with romantic love. But I think we should be able to say it more often to our family, our friends, and even our oshis, because we genuinely love and care about them. Not in a romantic way, but in a platonic one. We can love someone simply as a fellow human being. We can appreciate their presence, the way they dress, the hard work they put into what they do, or even the beauty of their souls. Sometimes, the little things someone shares with the world can make our own world a little brighter. Perhaps that's one of the beautiful things about being a fan. Even from a distance, someone can bring us happiness in ways they may never realize. A smile, a song, or simply seeing them do what they love can make an ordinary day feel a little more special. I hope you know that your presence means something to the people who support you, even in ways you may never fully understand. As you turn seventeen, I hope you remember that you don't have to be perfect, strong all the time, or have everything figured out. You're allowed to make mistakes, feel tired, and grow at your own pace. You deserve to enjoy your youth, discover new things, and become the person you want to be without rushing through life. And I think we also need to say “I love you” to ourselves more often. We've all worked so hard to get to where we are today. We've been through difficult days, faced our doubts, and continued trying even when things weren't easy. And yet, here we are, still growing, still trying, still alive. I hope you remember to give yourself credit for how far you've come. You are worthy of love not only when you succeed, when you make others happy, or when you shine on a stage. You deserve love on your quiet days, too, simply because you are you. For your seventeenth birthday, I hope life treats you gently. May you find more reasons to smile, people who make you feel safe, and little moments that remind you how beautiful it is to be alive. I hope your dreams bring you excitement, your journey gives you room to grow, and happiness finds you even in the simplest things. Thank you for being here, for doing what you love, and for sharing little pieces of yourself with us. I hope the happiness you've unknowingly given to others finds its way back to you, too. And if no one has told you this lately, let me be the one to say it: you don't have to do anything extraordinary to deserve love. You are enough, just as you are. Happy seventeenth birthday, once again. I hope this new chapter brings you beautiful memories, gentle days, and countless reasons to fall in love with life. Somewhere in this world, someone is cheering for you from afar and wishing you nothing but happiness.
With love,
izalcul. ♡
